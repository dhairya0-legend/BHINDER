Last updated: 10 October 2026

BHINDER (“Tinder Ka Bhai”) is a free parody comedy chat for adults. Everything here is fictional, and every character is an AI. By using the site you agree to the points below.

1. Privacy policy

What we collect

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, address, school or social media. Please don’t type any of that into the chat.

Your messages

The messages you type are sent to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) and then to Google’s Gemini AI so the character can reply. Your conversation lives in your browser while the page is open, and we do not intentionally save your chats or build profiles of users.

Usage limits

To stop spam and protect our free AI quota, we count how many messages are sent per minute and per day. This count is tied to a scrambled (hashed) ID made from your connection, not to your real IP address, name or identity.

Third parties

The site depends on GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare (server), Google Gemini (AI replies) and Google Fonts (fonts). These services may keep standard technical logs under their own policies. We do not use ads, analytics or tracking cookies of our own.

Children

This site is for adults 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please leave.
